## Onboarding Call Guide (20 Minutes)

### 0:00 — Intro (2 min)
- Welcome them by name, thank them for their time
- Quick context: "We're building SOLO SME for business owners like you. Today we'll set up your account and show you the 3 things you'll use every day."
- Set expectation: "This is beta — things might not be perfect. Your feedback is exactly what we need."

---

### 0:02 — Account Setup (5 min)
1. Ask them to open **solosme.ng** on their phone or laptop
2. Walk them through sign-up (email + OTP)
3. Have them add **1 product** to their store — name, price, stock quantity
4. Show them their **storefront link** — explain customers can order directly from this link

> Tip: If they struggle with the dashboard, just say "Don't worry — you can do everything from WhatsApp too."

---

### 0:07 — WhatsApp Setup (5 min)
1. Guide them to **Dashboard → Settings → WhatsApp**
2. Connect their WhatsApp Business number
3. Once connected, have them send **"MENU"** to the SOLO number
4. Show them the menu options — walk through what each one does in plain language

---

### 0:12 — First Live Action (5 min)
Pick ONE of these to do live with them:

**Option A — Record a Sale**
> "Just type: _Sold 2 [product name] [price]_ and send it"

**Option B — Check Stock**
> "Type: _How many [product name] do I have?_"

**Option C — Business Report**
> "Type: _Show me today's report_"

Let them do it themselves. Celebrate when it works. If it fails, note it — don't troubleshoot on the call.

---

### 0:17 — Feedback Expectations (3 min)
- "Over the next 7 days, just use it like your normal business tool"
- Ask them to WhatsApp us directly when something confuses them or breaks
- Give them the feedback number: **[YOUR WHATSAPP NUMBER]**
- Ask one question before hanging up: *"What's the one thing you wish this could do that you didn't see today?"*

---

## Things to Watch During the Call

| Signal | What it means |
|--------|---------------|
| They struggle to find a button | Navigation UX issue — note it |
| They ask "what does this mean?" | Copy/labels need work |
| They say "oh this is like [X app]" | Note the comparison |
| They hesitate before typing a command | Command format is not intuitive |
| They complete an action without help | That flow is solid |

---

## Common Questions & Answers

**"What if I don't use WhatsApp Business?"**
> Regular WhatsApp works for testing. You'll want WhatsApp Business for your actual store number eventually.

**"Can my customers order from WhatsApp too?"**
> Yes — share your storefront link and customers can place orders online. WhatsApp is for you to manage the business.

**"Is my data safe?"**
> Yes — everything is encrypted and stored securely. You can delete your account anytime.

**"When is the full launch?"**
> We're targeting [DATE]. Your feedback directly shapes what we fix before then.
